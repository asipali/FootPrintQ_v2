Security and governance

Security & Responsible Use Principles

Access controls and scope checks are built into application workflows; users remain responsible for lawful, authorized use.

01

Role-Based Access Control

Role and permission checks protect operational routes and administrative workflows.

02

Target-Scope Boundaries

Active Nmap and vulnerability assessment workflows require explicit authorization for the target.

03

Evidence Provenance & Auditing

Evidence records retain provenance metadata and audit events record important account and administrative activity.

04

Cryptographic Protections

Passwords are hashed, browser sessions are server-managed, and evidence integrity is represented with SHA-256 hashes.

05

Responsible Use Standard

Use FootprintIQ only for assets you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.

06

Investigator Approval

When public registration is enabled, new investigator accounts remain pending until an administrator approves them.
